How to show the feature working end to end.

  1. Record call one — as the sales rep, quote a product price to Z, e.g. “Basmati rice is $940 per tonne.”
  2. Drop the call — the conversation is stored with price_bas112 = $940.
  3. Click “Simulate overnight market move” — Basmati moves to $955 in the feed.
  4. Run the callback — during the ring window the freshness check sees $940 → $955 and the Rime recap says “Heads up — Basmati Rice price was $940, it's now $955.”
